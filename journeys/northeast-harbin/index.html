The Family Journey

Journey I · Planning

Northeast
China

A slow northern journey through Harbin, the black-soil plains, and the country of cranes.

In progress. This family journey is being shaped around late autumn, gentle pacing, four grandparents, and Bubu. Dates, routes, and stays will be refined together before they become final.

The working rhythm

I

Arrive in Harbin

Two families, from Shenzhen and Changsha, meet in the northern city. The first day is reserved for a warm meal and an early night.

II–IV

The city, slowly

Harbin’s streets, riverside, animals, Russian architecture, and long pauses for rest. One main experience a day is enough.

V–VI

Toward the crane country

A second, quieter city: open black-soil plains, crane wetlands, and a shared table of Northeast barbecue. The final route is still being considered.

VII–VIII

A gentle way home

Return to Harbin with room for weather, naps, luggage, and an unhurried airport morning.

One thing a day

We trade crowded checklists for one good memory, and leave room to follow the day.

Nature, within reach

Animals, wetlands, trees, and open sky—without asking little legs or older knees for too much.

Comfort is part of it

Warm meals, naps, familiar rhythms, and a soft landing matter as much as every destination.
